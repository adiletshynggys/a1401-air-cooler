Cooling train from CO shift to PSA

Wet syngas leaves the CO shift at 429 °C and is cooled in five steps. A-1401 is the air cooler that must condense most of the water. Upstream, E-1408 should bring the gas to 137 °C but today delivers about 167 °C. At that temperature the gas carries almost three times as much water vapour, and A-1401 has to condense it.

Plant data, hourly

Load the hourly export (columns timestamp, 1400TIA050, 1400TICA0055_pv, 1400TICA0055_mv, 1400PICA0017_mv, 1400FI010, comma or tab separated). The file stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Frozen data (the same values repeated for 24 h or more, from about 2026-03-12) and shutdowns (FI010 < 10,000 Nm³/h or TIA050 < 120 °C) are excluded.

Validation against design and plant data

Only two quantities are tuned: the fan scaling and the unknown fouling. Everything else comes from the drawings, the P&ID, the lab analysis and balance.py, or is a labelled assumption.
